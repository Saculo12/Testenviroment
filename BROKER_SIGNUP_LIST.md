high signal), then **brokers with public newsletter forms** (just fill out and
go), then **gated brokers** (require introduction or cold email — use the
template at the bottom).

## Cold-email template for gated brokers

Use this verbatim (adjust bracketed parts). Send to the multifamily IS lead
for each gated firm in §3 above.

> **Subject:** Buyer registration — multifamily acquisitions, SoCal + AZ
>
> Hi [First Name],
>
> I'm an active acquirer of multifamily assets in Southern California and
> Arizona. My current buy box:
>
> - **Geography:** Greater Phoenix MSA (incl. Tempe / Mesa / Chandler / Glendale),
>   LA County, Orange County, Inland Empire, San Diego County
> - **Size:** 50–250 units
> - **Price range:** $[X]M – $[Y]M
> - **Strategy:** [value-add / core-plus / opportunistic]
> - **Hold:** 5–10 years, agency-financed
>
> Could you add me to your distribution list for relevant offerings? Happy
> to provide proof-of-funds / sources statement and confirm investor
> qualifications if helpful.
>
> Best,
> [Your name]
> [Your firm / individual]
> [Phone] · [deals@yourdomain.com]

**Tips for getting on the A-list:**
- Include a real phone number. Brokers screen out email-only signups.
- Naming a specific buy box (size + geography + strategy) gets you into the
  primary distribution list. Vague signups go to the public blast tier.
- If you have prior closings, mention one. If not, mention POF backing.
- Reply quickly the first time they send you an OM — even a "thanks, will
  review" gets you flagged as engaged and bumps you up their list.

## Suggested signup order (do this over 2–3 days, not one sitting)

1. **Day 1 (30 min):** All 5 listing platforms in §1. Set saved searches.
2. **Day 1 (15 min):** All 6 brokers in §2 with public forms.
3. **Day 1 (5 min):** ABI Multifamily (§4) — most important AZ signup.
4. **Day 2 (15 min):** All 4 SoCal specialists in §5.
5. **Day 2 (45 min):** Send cold emails to the 6 gated brokers in §3, one
   at a time, customized for each market lead. Don't bulk-send.
6. **Day 3+:** Reply promptly to any responses. Brokers test responsiveness
   before promoting you to their A-list.

Expected steady-state volume once everyone has you on their lists:
**~20–60 emails per business day** to your dedicated inbox.
